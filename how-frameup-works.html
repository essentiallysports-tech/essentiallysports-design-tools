Three steps from idea to file

Every design follows the same path, whichever workspace you use.

Step 01

Choose a workspace

Each workspace has the approved sizes and layouts for one channel. Pick the channel, then the template inside it.

Social Media Covers, quotes, player stats, head-to-head, rankings, timelines and tweet cards. Carousels up to 5 slides. 1080 × 1350 · 10 post types YouTube Thumbnails with one image, a player cut-out over a background, or two players. Quote mode for interview clips. 1280 × 720 · 3 layouts + quote Newsletter Feature images, section headers, the desktop news layout, trivia and editable infographics for every send. 14 assets · 18 newsletter brands Reel Captions Captions from speech, an editable transcript, brand-safe caption styles and lower thirds for short videos. Short-form video

Starting from a story? Generate turns a brief into editable Social Media posts you can finish in the editor.

Step 02

Add your content

Everything you change shows on the canvas straight away. The right-hand panel only shows what the template you picked needs.

  • Type your text

    Each line of the headline becomes its own pill. Press Enter to start a new line.

  • Pick the sport and team

    Team colours are applied to pills and accents for you, so every design matches the club's palette.

  • Add a photo

    Upload your own or search the ES Image Pool for the latest approved images of a player, team or topic. Drag it on the canvas to move it and zoom to crop.

  • Fill a template

    Stats, rankings, trivia and infographics have a field for every value. Infographics show one card per player, with the stats side by side.

Changed your mind? Undo and Redo sit next to the page title, or use Ctrl Z and Ctrl Y.

Step 03

Export and publish

When it looks right, take it wherever it needs to go.

Export

Downloads a platform-ready file named after your headline, so it is easy to find later.

es-ig-warriors-win-again-slide-1.jpeg
Carousels in one go

On a Social Media carousel, Export offers every filled slide at once, in order.

Copy link

Name the image and get a permanent link to share in Slack, email or a CMS. Anyone with the link can view it.

Find it in the Gallery

Every exported Social Media post appears in the Gallery, newest first, for the whole team.

The brand rules are built in

You focus on the story. FrameUp keeps every design on brand.

Approved sizes

Every template exports at the size each platform expects, with sharp, high-resolution output.

ES fonts and pills

Headlines use the ES condensed typeface in the signature pill style, spaced the approved way.

Team colours

Pick a team and its colours are applied for you, across leagues and sports.

Logo and safe zones

The logo sits in its approved spot (choose ES, Essentially Golf or Essentially W on Social Media and YouTube), and key content stays clear of platform overlays.

Tips and shortcuts

Small things that save time every day.

Ctrl K or /
Search FrameUp and jump to any workspace or guide.
Ctrl Z
Undo the last change to your design.
Ctrl Y
Redo it (or Ctrl Shift Z).
Enter
In the headline box, start a new pill on its own line.
Drag on the canvas
Move a photo inside its frame; use Zoom to crop tighter.
Collapse the sidebar
The button next to the logo gives the canvas more room. FrameUp remembers your choice.

On a Mac, use ⌘ instead of Ctrl.
