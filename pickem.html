How Pick'em works

Pick the winner of every NFL matchup. One point for every correct pick—incorrect and missed picks earn zero. Tied or canceled games are void and don't count.

Locking

Every game locks independently at its own official kickoff. You can still change any pick that hasn't locked yet, right up until that game starts—even if other games from earlier in the week are already locked.

Tiebreakers

To settle weekly ties, predict the exact final score of the primary tiebreaker game (usually Monday Night Football). If that game is void, the backup game's prediction is used instead. Players tied on correct picks with no valid tiebreaker rank behind players who submitted one.

Joining late

You can join any time during the week. “Complete” only measures the games still available to you at that moment—games that already locked before you joined are shown as Missed, but don't block completion.

Leaderboards

Weekly and season leaderboards use competition ranking: if two players are tied, they share the same rank and the next rank number is skipped.
