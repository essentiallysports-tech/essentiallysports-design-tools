Contrast

Body text 4.5:1, large text and non-text 3:1, in both themes.

  • White on Blue 6004.8:1. The blue that takes a white label.
  • White on Blue 5003.9:1. Large text only; never a button label.
  • Blue 400 on Black7.3:1 (6.2:1 on Slate 800). Blue text on dark only: on white it is 2.6:1.
  • Brand text on white5.9:1 (--es-brand-text).
  • Ink 3 on white6.7:1; Slate 200 on Slate 800 is 6.6:1.
DoFill buttons and badges with Blue 600 when the label is white.
Don'tUse Blue 400 for text on white, or any purple or violet in UI chrome.

Buttons

Four variants, three sizes, one family. Everything you see comes from the @layer es-buttons block in design-tokens.css; page CSS sets layout only.

Variants, sizes and states

One animated primary per view. Repeats of the key action are secondary or .es-btn--static. Everything else is secondary; tertiary for low emphasis; danger for destructive actions.

VariantSmall · 32Default · 40Large · 48FocusDisabled
PrimaryThe key action. Moving rim.
SecondaryEvery other action.
TertiaryLow emphasis. Alias --ghost.
DangerDestructive.
Busy (.is-busy or aria-busy, primary only): the rim keeps turning and a sheen sweeps across. Ignore clicks, keep the label.

Hover: secondary darkens its line and fills Sunk; tertiary fills Sunk; danger darkens to the label red; primary brightens its rim and adds --ais-glow. Press: scale .96 (not when disabled or with reduced motion). Touch: small and default grow to 44px. Radius 10 · Roboto 500.

Primary in light and dark

Only colours change between themes. Light: white fill, ink label, a deeper blue rim. Dark: ES Black fill, white label, the bright rim.

Focus and selection

One quiet focus system, defined once in design-tokens.css, and one calm selected style.

Focus

Fields: the outline turns ES blue (same width) plus one soft halo; no outline ring. Buttons, links, chips, tabs: keyboard only (:focus-visible), one 2px outline with a 2px offset. A mouse click shows nothing.

Control, keyboard focus

Values: light #0A6FDE (Blue 600; 4.8:1 on white) with a 3px halo at 16%; dark #54A4FB (Blue 400; 7.2:1 on Black) with a 3px halo at 22%. Offset 2px in both.

DoUse the tokens and let the global rule handle buttons, links and tabs.
Don'tStack an outline and a halo, show a ring on mouse click, or remove focus with no replacement.

Selected

Selection is calm: a white pill on a grey track for segments, a soft blue tint for chips (a gradient tint on AI pages), a white pill with a blue icon for the current nav item.

Forms

White fields with a 1px Line 2 outline, Ink 3 on hover, blue plus halo on focus. 44px tall, with 16px text on phones so iOS never zooms.

Fields

Label above, 8px to the control; helper or error text 8px below. Placeholders show an example, never the instruction.

Search

Tabs

Underline tabs switch views inside a page: 15/500, the active one gets primary text and a 2px blue line that slides. Counts are 13px muted, blue when something is new.

Step rail

For guided flows of 3–5 steps. Current: filled ink (white in dark). Done: a blue check, clickable to go back. Upcoming: outline. Phones show "Step 2 of 3 · Clip" with a thin bar.

Step 2 of 3 · Clip

.es-steps (in Motion above) is the compact shared version for inline progress.

Segmented control

For 2–4 mutually exclusive options that change the same view (size, mode, tone, count). More than four, or different views, use tabs. One height everywhere: 36px segments in a 4px grey track (44px), 10px segment radius, 14px track radius, on every page (Generate, Trends, Video Ads, Reels Studio, Home). Selected follows the ARIA the control already uses: aria-checked (role=radio, arrow keys move), aria-selected (tabs), aria-pressed or .is-active. Icon-only segments add --icon and a title. --sm (32px segments in a 3px track, 38px) is for the dense editor inspector only.

Chips

Filters and quick choices: .es-chip everywhere (Home, Generate, Trends, Video Ads, Reels Studio). 32px tall (44px on touch screens), 10px radius, 1px outline, Roboto 500 13. Pressed or checked: the calm blue tint (a gradient tint on AI pages). A league logo, team logo or colour dot leads the label; a count sits after it in .es-chip__count. Toggle filters use aria-pressed; one-of choices use role=radio + aria-checked. Don't make pill-shaped (999px) chips; pills are for badges.

    Layout

    One shell, one content column, three breakpoints.

    Shell and content width

    The 224px sidebar on the left; content in one column of up to 1120px with a 32px gutter (16px on phones). The page title sits 32px from the top.

    Breakpoints

    Design at these three widths and check there is never sideways page scroll; wide rows scroll inside themselves.

    375Phone. The sidebar becomes a menu under the top bar; 16px gutter; one column; 44px touch targets; 16px field text.
    900The shell switch. Above it, the fixed sidebar and the 32px gutter; at or below it, the phone layout.
    1280Laptop. Content column up to 1120px; 3–4 column grids; the Stars ticker tightens its label below it.

    Light and dark

    Every page supports both. The switch sets html[data-theme] and remembers it; components change colour only, never size or spacing.
