Buttons

Four variants, three sizes, one family. One primary per view; everything else is secondary, tertiary or danger.

VariantSmall · 32Default · 40Large · 48Disabled
PrimaryThe key action. Moving ES rim.
SecondaryEvery other action.
TertiaryLow emphasis.
DangerDestructive.
Busy primary: the rim keeps turning and a sheen sweeps across.

Radius 10px · Roboto 500 · icons 16px (small) or 18px, 8px gap, always the label colour · focus: 2px blue outline, 2px offset · press: scale .96 · touch screens: small and default grow to 44px. Light and dark change colours only. The primary is white with an ink label in light mode and ES Black with a white label in dark mode. .es-btn--still is for specimens on this page only: the primary look without motion.
